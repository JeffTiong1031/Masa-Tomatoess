## Type

Geist only. Saturday can be large (`text-5xl` to `text-7xl`) but stays Geist semibold cocoa. No serif. No Space Grotesk. No Clash Display.

## Structure

1. Cream field, lively macaron backdrop (see below).
2. White 44px circular menu chip, cocoa Menu icon.
3. Quiet greeting: Good evening, Jeff. 47 focus minutes today.
4. Huge Saturday. Date 12 September 2026. Agenda: 09:00 Tutorial, 14:30 Groceries, 19:00 Dinner.
5. White month card, calendar-pink today mark, accent-tinted shadow.
6. Today 47 min tile (timer coral). Streak banner with chevrons.
7. **All eight section doors stay visible** in one wrapping row. Do not drop any. Do not paint them all one colour.

Door wells, 44px rounded-xl, each its own accent:

## Lively backdrop (this is the colour work)

Keep cream as the field. Add **many more elements**, not new hues. Six blobs is not enough.

Allowed decorations, all from the accents above:

- Large soft blobs (opacity 0.16 to 0.32)
- Medium ovals
- Small unblurred dots and squircle chips scattered at the edges
- Thin pastel rings
- A faint `#F0E4DA` mosaic

No wallpaper, no dark scrim, no forest green, no gold, no cobalt.

## Right-column cards must not be empty

The month card, Today tile, and streak banner cannot be plain white + one number. Each needs inner pieces: a tinted header or wash, a Lucide well, chips or pips, and a second line of copy. Cards stay white or lightly tinted with an existing accent at 12 to 22 percent. Radius `1.5rem`. Shadow tinted by that card's accent.

## Icons

Lucide outlines, stroke 1.9, cocoa. Every door keeps its own pastel well.

## Do not

- Replace the palette.
- Hide or merge the eight doors.
- Recolour every well forest / gold / cobalt.
