# Finance — by-hand checks

Run [2026-10-02-finance-setup.sql](../specs/2026-10-02-finance-setup.sql) in the
Supabase SQL editor first. Do these on a phone, signed in as yourself.

## First visit

- [ ] Finance shows "Let's get started" with Add categories and Set budget.
- [ ] Add categories opens the Categories sheet. "Saving" is already listed
      under Income with a lock and no rename or delete.
- [ ] Add a few expense categories (Food, Transport, Bills) and one income
      category (Salary).
- [ ] Set a weekly budget. The bar appears, starting today.

## Adding

- [ ] The "+" stays in the bottom-right while you scroll and does not hide
      behind the phone's home bar.
- [ ] The add sheet opens with the keypad ready; tapping digits shows RM 12.50.
- [ ] Category chips sit within thumb reach. After a few entries, the
      category you use most moves to the front.
- [ ] Save shows "Saved ✓", clears the amount and note, and stays open.
- [ ] Yesterday and Pick date work; Pick date will not go past today.
- [ ] Type a note, then "Save as quick add". The chip appears, and one tap
      adds that entry. Edit → × deletes it.
- [ ] "+ New" in the sheet makes a category on the spot.

## Editing and deleting

- [ ] Tap an entry: the sheet opens filled in; change the amount; Save closes it.
- [ ] Swipe an entry left about a third of the way: it disappears and
      "Deleted · Undo" shows for about 5 seconds. Undo brings it back.
- [ ] A short swipe springs back. Scrolling the list up and down does not
      trigger a swipe.
- [ ] Saving entries show "Auto", cannot be tapped and cannot be swiped.

## Budget bar

- [ ] Reads "RM X left · N days left · ~RM Y/day" on two lines.
- [ ] Bar turns amber at 25% left or less, red once you go over, and the
      words change to "RM X over".
- [ ] Change the amount: the bar updates at once.
- [ ] Switch Week → Month: a note under the bar says when the monthly budget
      starts; this week keeps its amount until then.

## Month picker and views

- [ ] Arrows and a sideways swipe change month; both ends stop.
- [ ] Daily shows newest day first with each day's total; income shows as
      "+RM … in".
- [ ] Summary shows each expense category with RM, % and a bar, then Total
      spent and Total income.

## Over time (check across a period end)

- [ ] The morning after a period ends, opening Finance adds one Saving entry
      dated the period's last day. Reloading does not add a second.
- [ ] Adding a forgotten expense with "Yesterday" on the first day of a new
      period changes yesterday's period's Saving entry, not today's bar.
- [ ] Open Finance on both phones at the same moment after a period ends:
      only one Saving entry appears.
- [ ] Rachel signed in sees none of Jeff's categories, entries or budget.
